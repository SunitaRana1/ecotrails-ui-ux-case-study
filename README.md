**Tools:** Figma (Design Systems, Auto Layout, Interactive Prototyping)  
**Domain:** Sustainable Tourism, Trekking & Carbon Footprint Tracking

## 📌 Project Overview
**EcoTrails** is an eco-conscious travel and trail discovery web platform designed to connect mindful travelers with low-impact Himalayan treks, community-run eco-stays across Nepal, and actionable carbon-footprint tracking.

## 🎯 Problem & Design Goal
* **The Problem:** While interest in sustainable travel is growing, travelers lack a unified platform that combines authentic eco-tourism experiences with transparent carbon-impact calculation and Leave No Trace education.
* **The Solution:** A structured, editorial web experience featuring verified eco-getaways, detailed multi-day trail itineraries (e.g., *5 Day Poonhill Trail*), an interactive carbon footprint calculator, and practical sustainability guides.

## 🛠️ End-to-End UX Process
1. **User Research & Personas:** Identified core user needs around trust, conservation transparency, and accessible eco-planning for conscious travelers.
2. **Information Architecture & User Flows:** Mapped intuitive navigation across *Discover Trails*, *Green Experiences*, *Nature Journal*, *Sustainability*, and *About*.
3. **Wireframing:** Built low-fidelity layouts to establish content hierarchy for multi-day trek itineraries, FAQ accordions, and carbon offset guides.
4. **Design System & Component Library:** Engineered a reusable Figma component library (`navbarDropdownComponents`, hero carousels, itinerary cards, eco-pledge grids) built on an accessible **Alpine Pine (`#316B43`)** and **Marigold Amber (`#F3A833`)** color system paired with **Playfair Display** and **Montserrat** typography.

## 🖥️ Key Pages Designed
* **Homepage:** Editorial hero banner, featured trail stories, conservation impact metrics, and traveler reflections.
* **5-Day Poonhill Trail:** Altitude/grade overview cards, eco-friendly practices, day-by-day itinerary breakdown, and photo gallery.
* **Eco-Getaways Across Nepal:** Curated community eco-lodges and sustainable farm stays across Bandipur, Nuwakot, Pokhara, Bardia, and Sindhupalchowk.
* **Carbon Footprint & Offset Guide:** Interactive carbon calculator entry point, 7 natural offset strategies, and lifestyle emission breakdowns.
* **Leave No Trace & Sustainable Gear:** Visual 7-principle guide, eco-friendly gear comparison table, and single-use plastic swap recommendations.
